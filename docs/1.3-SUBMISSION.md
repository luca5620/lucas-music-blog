## App Review Information → Notes

Sign-in information: the `applereview` account is already in ASC from
1.2 — **log in with it on the live app on submit day** (playbook rule)
before trusting it. Never write its password in this repo.

```
Peak Music Reviews is a free music social network: users rate and review albums and songs (0–10), build lists, join live release-day chat rooms and song-vs-song "Aux Wars" rooms, and follow each other.

DEMO ACCOUNT: in the sign-in fields above (registration is also open: email + username, with an emailed confirmation link, or Sign in with Apple / Google).

WHERE THINGS ARE: bottom tabs Home / Taste / Create (+) / Social / Profile. Create makes reviews, posts, lists and rooms.

USER-GENERATED CONTENT SAFEGUARDS (Guideline 1.2):
- Signup requires agreeing to the Terms/EULA (zero tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users).
- All user text is filtered for slurs and harassment before posting. GIFs come from KLIPY with its strictest content rating.
- Every review, comment, post, list, chat message, room and profile has a report button (flag icon) that goes to our moderation queue, reviewed within 24 hours. Staff can remove reported text, avatars and banners.
- Block is on every profile: it notifies us, unfollows both ways, and removes the blocked user's content from every feed immediately.
- Room hosts can remove and ban participants.

ACCOUNT DELETION: Profile tab → Settings → Delete Account (permanent).

NATIVE FEATURES: push notifications (a record you saved comes out, Song of the Day streak reminder, weekly recap, this week's Community Spotlight), in-app Sign in with Apple and Google, one-tap sharing to Instagram Stories and TikTok, haptics, native share sheet, offline screen, native splash, iPhone Duo inner-screen support.

SHARING: Share to Instagram Stories / TikTok opens those apps with the user's own review card or video; there is no login to those services and nothing is posted without the user finishing it in that app.

THIRD-PARTY CONTENT: release metadata and artwork come from the official Spotify and Genius APIs; previews use Spotify's 30-second previews and official embeds. The app stores and streams no full-length audio.

SEASONAL: a holiday look (snow, string lights, a Santa hat on the mascot) shows to everyone until December 30, then turns itself off. Peak Music Rewind (a yearly recap) opens December 1 for members who rated 3+ records this year.

WHAT'S NEW IN 1.3: built with the iOS 27.1 SDK for iPhone Duo, plus one-tap Instagram Stories / TikTok sharing.
```

⚠️ Check before pasting: the moderation promise ("within 24 hours") must
be one Luca can keep; the account-deletion path must match the app on
submit day (Settings → Profile page → **Delete Account**).
