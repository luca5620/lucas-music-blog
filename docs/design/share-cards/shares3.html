Share cards: round three

All three now carry the penguin logo with Peak Music in the PS3 font. The sticker and the scorecard take their colours from the album cover and both move; the two .mp4 files sit next to this image. My month is unchanged apart from the logo.

▶ moving

1 · The stickerSee-through glass tinted with the cover's own colours, which slowly swirl inside it, so every album gets its own sticker. The structure is ours too: logo top-left, the cover with a record peeking out, and the score in the rating colour. Over someone's reaction video, their video shows through the glass. File: share-sticker-preview.mp4.

2 · The album scorecard (video)The record spins with the cover as its centre label, and the background drifts in the cover's colours, like the website's liquid. Every score rolls like an odometer, the digits spinning and passing through the rating colours, then the album score rolls up, overshoots a touch and settles with a glow.
