# Duolingo: what they actually do (researched 2026-10-25)

Treat vendor-blog numbers as directional; the figures below come from
Duolingo's former CPO (Lenny's Newsletter), shareholder-letter quotes, and
The Drum / Technical.ly interviews unless marked.

## The scale (for context, not comparison)

- Daily users 8.2M (2020) → 56.5M (Q1 2026); DAU/MAU ~41%, a
  social-network number for an education app.
- 10M+ people have a year-long streak; ~1 in 3 daily users has a Friend
  Streak.
- Sales + marketing was ~12% of revenue in FY2024. **Most growth is the
  product, not paid ads.**

## 1. The growth turnaround (2018–2022): product, not marketing

Daily-user growth had slowed to single digits by mid-2018. The fix:

- **Growth model**: every user in exactly one bucket per day (new, current,
  reactivated, resurrected, at-risk WAU, at-risk MAU, dormant). Simulated
  nudging each rate 2%/quarter for 3 years. **Current-user retention moved
  DAU ~5x more than any other lever** → a Retention Team with that as its
  North Star (they deliberately deprioritised new-user retention).
- **Leagues** (from FarmVille 2, simplified, opt-in by default): +17%
  learning time, 3x highly engaged learners.
- **Notifications**: free to test copy/timing/images; *more volume* needed
  CEO approval (Groupon's email burnout was the cautionary tale). A bandit
  algorithm picks templates. Dozens of small wins compounding.
- **Streaks**: the late-night streak-saver push was the first big win;
  then freeze, calendar, animations, rewards. Share of DAU with a 7+ day
  streak nearly tripled to over half.
- Result: current-user retention +21%, daily churn of best users −40%+,
  DAU 4.5x over four years.

**Two flops worth remembering:** a Gardenscapes-style moves counter
(neutral, a "tacked-on nuisance") and an Uber-style referral giving a free
month of Plus (+3% new users, because the best advocates already paid).

## 2. Social: the unhinged owl (2021–2025)

- Started with **commenting on other people's TikToks** before posting
  their own. The owl suit was lying around from college-recruiting events.
- **Tiny team**: two full-time people plus a few contractors; weekly
  brainstorm around trending audio; a two-day production cycle; the team
  approved its own posts without leadership sign-off.
- **Mascot as a character** with relationships and story arcs, built on
  the in-app joke (passive-aggressive reminders). "A lot of our social is
  just what our community wants us to do." (Zaria Parvez, The Drum.)
- **Always reply in comments**, trend-jack pop culture (Barbie, Bad Bunny).
- 50K TikTok followers (Sept 2021) → ~16–17M.
- **"Duo is dead" (Feb 2025)**: a multi-week storyline; ~1B organic views,
  near-zero paid media. The revival was gated on users collectively
  earning 50B XP *in the app*: earned media tied back to the habit.
- The stated reason it works: "when you see an owl twerking on TikTok, the
  first thing that comes to mind is 'Oh, I haven't done my Duolingo today'."

## 3. The backlash and the pullback (2025–2026)

- April 2025 "AI-first" memo from the CEO → comment sections overrun;
  Duolingo wiped posts and reportedly lost 400K+ TikTok followers.
- They stopped posting edgy content to repair sentiment. The Q3 2025
  shareholder letter said DAU growth slowed partly because they "posted
  less 'unhinged' content". DAU growth went 54% (Q3'24) → 21% (Q1'26).
- 2026: CMO talks about fewer butt jokes, a more balanced mix; Duo's
  birthday campaign let users **restore their longest lost streak** (the
  community had asked for years); Super Bowl LX skipped an ~$8M spot for
  an in-app "Bad Bunny 101" module plus cheaper placements.
- **Ads inside Duolingo**: a new opt-in "Pre-Lesson Energy Sponsorship":
  watch a 30s video before your first lesson → 30 minutes of unlimited
  Energy. Rewarded, opt-in, tied to something the user wants.

**Lessons:** a brand voice is an asset that a single off-brand moment can
cost you; the community will police authenticity; ads work best when the
user chooses them in exchange for something.

## 4. What to borrow for Peak Music (and what not)

| Duolingo move | Reason it works there | Peak Music version |
|---|---|---|
| Streak + streak-saver push | Daily-sized action | Song of the Day streak (exists) + one evening saver push; weekly rating streak |
| Leagues | Lots of weekly actives | Not yet (too few actives); "top raters this week" at most |
| Notification discipline | Channel is fragile | Social pushes yes; nagging no; cap and back off |
| Mascot as character | In-app joke to amplify | **The penguin**: give it a personality on socials (laid-back music snob, never a "PEAK TV" bit). Needs no face or voice from Luca |
| Comment-first social | Cheap reach, community voice | Reply on music TikToks and Reels as Peak Music (the brand account), especially leaks/unreleased creators |
| Storyline stunts | Earned media | Small: "the penguin is rating every album in [artist] discography this week", ending in a share card |
| Year in Review | Personal + shareable | December "Peak Music Rewind" from ratings data |
| Restore lost streaks (birthday) | Goodwill + reactivation | App birthday (launch was 2026-08-31): restore SOTD streaks for a week |
| Opt-in rewarded ad | User chooses the trade | See web-monetization skill (web only, never manipulative) |
| AI-first memo | — | Don't. Peak Music's audience already calls things "vibecoded"; never brag about AI building it |
