## Ground rules for the images

- **Format:** 4:5 portrait (1080×1350) for feed posts; 9:16 for stories
  and reels covers. Say the ratio in every prompt.
- **One look, every post.** Black background, CRT scanlines and film
  grain, electric-blue glow (#1e90ff), chrome and liquid-light accents,
  VHS-label typography. It's the site; the grid should look like the
  site.
- **Text in AI images is unreliable.** Keep any in-image text to ONE
  short line (2–4 words) and check the spelling. Longer text goes in
  the caption or gets added afterwards in Canva.
- **No real artists, no real album covers, no logos** in generated
  images — likeness/copyright risk, and the models refuse anyway. Use
  abstract "album cover" shapes. Real releases live in the caption.
- **Mix in real screenshots.** AI images are the hook; the second and
  third slides of a carousel should be actual phone screenshots
  (release room, debate, a themed profile). Product photos convert,
  mood images stop the scroll.
- **Prompt shape that works in ChatGPT:** subject → composition →
  style/materials → lighting → what text (if any) → ratio → "no other
  text, no watermarks, no logos".
