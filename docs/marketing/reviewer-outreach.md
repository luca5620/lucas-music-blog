# Reviewer outreach kit (2026-09-09)

Luca's channel, Luca's idea: email YouTube music reviewers and reactors
one at a time and ask them to try Peak Music Reviews. Not a shout-out
request. The ask is always the same sentence: **"if you like it, let me
know."** We market a finished app, not a project — nothing in these
emails says beta, early, or broken.

Three rules, all standing:

1. **Send them all in one sitting.** (Luca 2026-09-09: all at once is
   fine.) Each email is already written for its channel, so there is
   nothing to learn from one reply before the next goes out.
2. **The proof link is your own profile.** Nobody clicks a homepage to
   understand a rating app; they click a page with forty records on it.
   Before the first email goes out, your profile has at least forty
   ratings on it (score only is fine — that's what the review form's
   backfill loop is for, shipped e0e6649).
3. **Never mention a competitor.** Not Musicboard, not RYM, not
   Letterboxd. The Reddit lesson: the thread becomes about them.

## What to link

- **Your profile** — the page they'd have. Screenshot it too; some
  people read email on a phone and won't tap.
- **One release page with TRACK RATINGS open** — for the two
  track-by-track channels this is the whole pitch.
- **The App Store link** only as a footnote. The web works without an
  install; the install is a bigger ask than a click.

Sign as Luca, first name only, with the site in the signature. Send
from contact@peakmusicreviews.com so a reply lands where you'll see it.

## Where the addresses are

Every YouTube channel has an **About** page with a "View email

## Before the first email goes out

Walk this in order. It's the exact path the reviewer takes.

- [ ] **Your profile has 40+ ratings.** Open the review form, publish,
      it stays open for the next one — the backfill loop. Score only.
- [ ] **Your profile theme is set** and the four favorites are filled.
      An empty showcase reads as an empty app.
- [ ] **A release page with real track ratings** — rate every track on
      two or three albums you know cold, so the RATINGS pane has
      numbers in it. Pick one of those as the release link.
- [ ] **Open the site signed out on your phone** and tap the profile
      link from the email. Confirm it loads in under three seconds and
      the covers aren't blank.
- [ ] **Sign up with a throwaway email** and check the path: signup →
      confirmation → home shows RATE YOUR FIRST RECORD (66b0967) →
      review form → publish → the strip appears and the search box is
      back. If any step feels slow or confusing, that's a fix before
      the send, not after.
- [ ] **contact@ inbox works** and you'll see a reply the same day.

## When they reply

- **"Looks cool, I'll check it out"** — reply with one line of thanks
  and nothing else. Don't send more links.
- **They signed up** — you'll see the account. Follow them from your
  profile. Do NOT DM them about it. If they post about it, that's
  theirs to do.
- **A bug** — fix it, reply "fixed" with no essay. Fast fixes are the
  best marketing to a person who already tried it.
- **A feature request** — write it in ROADMAP, reply "noted, thanks."
  Don't promise a date.
